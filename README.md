# duties-and-dragons
A dark fantasy-themed task manager where users complete daily tasks to weaken and eventually defeat a virtual boss. Inspired by Google Tasks, but with a twist: every task completed is a strike against the forces of chaos.

## Features 
**Task Management:** Add, edit, and delete tasks with deadlines.

**Boss Health System:** Each completed task reduces the boss's health.

**Dark Fantasy Theme:** Immersive UI with gothic and fantasy elements.

**Progress Tracking:** Visualize your progress toward defeating the boss

## How to Use
**Add a Task:** Click the "Add Task" button and describe your task.

**Set a Deadline:** Assign a due date to prioritize your quests.

**Complete Tasks:** Mark tasks as done to deal damage to the boss.

**Defeat the Boss:** Watch the boss's health bar deplete as you conquer your to-do list!!
